SERVER RULES

Devs Anarchy is built around freedom and anarchy. However, certain actions are strictly prohibited to protect our players, community, and infrastructure.

OFFICIAL RULES

The Rules

These rules apply to the Devs Anarchy Minecraft server, Discord community, website, and related services.

01

No Illegal Content

Illegal content is strictly prohibited. You may not upload, send, distribute, request, store, link to, or otherwise share illegal content through any Devs Anarchy service.

This rule heavily applies to anything involving minors. Any sexual, exploitative, abusive, or otherwise illegal content involving minors is strictly prohibited.

Do not attempt to use Devs Anarchy, its Discord, Minecraft server, website, or staff channels to distribute or request illegal material.

02

No DDoSing

DDoS attacks and denial-of-service attacks are strictly prohibited.

Do not attempt to overload, disrupt, crash, or intentionally interfere with the Devs Anarchy server, website, Discord, network, hosting infrastructure, or another player's connection.

This includes using or coordinating third-party services to attack or disrupt another person or Devs Anarchy infrastructure.

03

No Doxing

Doxing is strictly prohibited.

You may not publish, threaten to publish, request, trade, or distribute another person's private or identifying information without their permission.

This includes information such as real names, home addresses, phone numbers, private email addresses, passwords, financial information, private account information, or other sensitive personal information.

Threatening someone with the release of their personal information is also prohibited.

⚠ ZERO TOLERANCE

These rules are not optional. Severe violations may result in immediate removal from the server or community, permanent bans, removal of purchased privileges, and reports to the appropriate platform or authorities when required.

Devs Anarchy reserves the right to take action against behavior that presents a serious threat to players, the community, or our infrastructure, even if a situation is not explicitly described above.

HAVE A QUESTION?

Need Help?

If you believe a rule has been violated or you have a question about these rules, contact the Devs Anarchy support team.
