PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how Devs Anarchy may collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website, Minecraft server, Discord community, and related services.

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Devs Anarchy. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information associated with your use of our services.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you visit devsanarchy.xyz, play on the Devs Anarchy Minecraft server, interact with our Discord community, purchase products or ranks, or contact our support team.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with Devs Anarchy, we may collect or receive the following information:

  • Minecraft information: Minecraft username, UUID, rank information, server activity, and related gameplay information.
  • Discord information: Discord username, user ID, messages, and information you choose to provide when interacting with our Discord server or staff.
  • Contact information: Email address and other information you provide when contacting support.
  • Purchase information: Information necessary to process purchases, such as transaction details or order information. Payment card information may be processed by third-party payment providers rather than stored directly by Devs Anarchy.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser information, device information, approximate location, and website usage information may be collected automatically.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing and maintaining the Devs Anarchy website and server.
  • Managing player accounts, ranks, purchases, and permissions.
  • Providing customer and technical support.
  • Preventing cheating, abuse, fraud, exploits, and other harmful activity.
  • Investigating security incidents and server issues.
  • Communicating important service announcements.
  • Improving website and server performance.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Minecraft Server Information

When you connect to the Devs Anarchy Minecraft server, certain information may be logged as part of normal server operation.

This can include your Minecraft username, UUID, IP address, connection information, gameplay activity, commands, punishments, and other information necessary to operate, secure, and moderate the server.

Server logs may be retained for security, moderation, troubleshooting, and administrative purposes.

5. Discord

Devs Anarchy uses Discord as a community and communication platform. If you join or interact with our Discord server, information may be processed by both Devs Anarchy and Discord according to their respective policies.

Please avoid sharing passwords, payment information, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in public Discord channels.

6. Payments and Purchases

Devs Anarchy may offer ranks, subscriptions, digital products, or other services through third-party payment providers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Devs Anarchy does not intentionally store complete payment card numbers or security codes.

Important: Never send your full credit card number, password, authentication code, or other sensitive payment information to Devs Anarchy through email, Discord, Minecraft, or our website.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies, local storage, analytics, or similar technologies to provide functionality, improve performance, maintain security, or understand how visitors use the website.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

Devs Anarchy may rely on third-party services to operate certain parts of the website, server, community, or store. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include hosting providers, payment processors, Discord, analytics providers, security services, and other infrastructure providers.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce our policies, maintain server records, or comply with legal requirements.

Different types of information may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose.

11. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with you, including the ability to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

Requests may be submitted to:

support@devsanarchy.xyz

12. Children's Privacy

Devs Anarchy does not knowingly request or intentionally collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe that information relating to a child has been provided to us may contact our support team.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices, or legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be changed.
