FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Devs Anarchy.

What version does Devs Anarchy use?

The server currently runs on Minecraft 26.2, but players on all versions can play.

Can I play on Bedrock?

Bedrock support is currently coming soon.

Can cracked players join?

Cracked support is currently coming soon.

What commands are available?

Players currently have access to commands such as /sit, /lay, and the home commands.

Are ranks available?

Ranks are currently coming soon. The planned ranks are VIP, MVP, ELITE, LEGEND and OVERLORD.

What do rank purchases support?

Rank purchases help pay for the server's hosting and operating costs.
