REFUND POLICY

This policy explains how refunds, cancellations, subscriptions, digital ranks, and other purchases made through the Devs Anarchy store are handled.

LAST UPDATED: August 27, 2026

[ REFUND_01 ]

Refund Policy Overview

Devs Anarchy may offer digital products including ranks, subscriptions, perks, cosmetics, and other digital entitlements.

Because these products are digital and may be delivered immediately, refunds are generally handled on a case-by-case basis.

Please review the product description and purchase details carefully before completing a transaction.
[ REFUND_02 ]

Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered when there is a legitimate issue with a purchase.

✓

MAY QUALIFY

Duplicate purchases or certain technical delivery problems may be reviewed.

⚠

CASE BY CASE

Some disputes may require additional information before a decision is made.

✕

GENERALLY NOT

Used digital products or voluntary purchases generally may not qualify.

A refund is not guaranteed simply because a player changes their mind after purchasing a digital product.
[ REFUND_03 ]

Digital Products

Devs Anarchy store products are digital services or digital entitlements.

Ranks

Rank purchases may provide access to specific in-game permissions, commands, homes, cosmetics, or other advertised features.

Digital Delivery

Digital purchases may be delivered automatically or manually after payment is confirmed.

If you paid for a product but did not receive it, contact Devs Anarchy support before opening a payment dispute.
[ REFUND_04 ]

Subscriptions

Certain Devs Anarchy products may be offered as recurring subscriptions.

Cancellation

If a subscription is offered, cancellation should be completed through the payment provider or subscription management system associated with the purchase.

Recurring Payments

By starting a subscription, you authorize recurring charges according to the terms displayed at checkout.

Canceling a subscription does not necessarily create an automatic refund for a previous completed billing period.
[ REFUND_05 ]

Duplicate Purchases

If you accidentally purchase the same digital product multiple times, contact support as soon as possible.

We may review duplicate transactions and, where appropriate, issue a refund or otherwise correct the duplicated purchase.

When contacting support, provide the transaction information necessary to identify the duplicate purchase.
[ REFUND_06 ]

Technical Problems

If you experience a problem receiving a purchased product, please contact Devs Anarchy support.

Examples

  • A purchased rank does not appear on the intended Minecraft account.
  • A purchased digital entitlement was not delivered.
  • A transaction was completed but the store did not properly process the purchase.
We may attempt to correct technical delivery problems before processing a refund.
[ REFUND_07 ]

Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you believe a transaction was incorrect, please contact Devs Anarchy support before initiating a chargeback whenever possible.

Unauthorized or fraudulent payment disputes may result in temporary or permanent restriction of associated accounts while the transaction is investigated.

Abuse of chargebacks or intentionally fraudulent payment disputes may result in loss of access to purchased digital products and Devs Anarchy services.
[ REFUND_08 ]

Fraud & Unauthorized Purchases

If you believe someone used your payment method without authorization, contact your payment provider immediately and notify Devs Anarchy support.

Devs Anarchy may investigate suspicious transactions and may temporarily restrict related accounts while an investigation is conducted.

[ REFUND_09 ]

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed through the applicable payment provider used for the original transaction whenever possible.

The time required for a refund to appear in your account depends on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, or other financial institution.

Devs Anarchy cannot control the exact processing time of a third-party payment provider.
[ REFUND_10 ]

Changes to This Policy

Devs Anarchy may update this Refund Policy when necessary to reflect changes to our store, payment systems, products, or operating practices.

Updates will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be changed accordingly.

[ REFUND_11 ]

Contact Support

If you believe you are eligible for a refund or are experiencing an issue with a purchase, contact the Devs Anarchy support team.

When contacting support, include as much relevant information as possible, such as your Minecraft username, transaction information, purchase date, and a description of the issue.

Do not send your payment card number, password, or other sensitive credentials through Discord or Minecraft chat.
